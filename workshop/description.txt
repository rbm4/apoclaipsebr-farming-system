[h1]ApocalipseBR - Farming System[/h1]

A server-side farming performance mod for Project Zomboid Build 42, designed for multiplayer servers with large farms.

Spreads plant maintenance across server ticks to help reduce farming-related lag spikes. Plants catch up on growth, water, health and disease as they are processed, and direct farming interactions coalesce the sync for the same plant at most once per tick, instead of per-command. These are Huge saving for servers with plants that have modData attached to them

[h2]Installation[/h2]

Subscribe and enable [b]ApocalypseBR_FarmingSystem[/b] in your server's mod list.

[h2]Settings and Compatibility[/h2]

[list]
[*]Adjust the farming time budget and update intervals in Sandbox Options.
[*]Adds no crops, items, recipes or farming UI.
[*]Checked against Build 42.21.0+
[*]Changes update scheduling; heavily overloaded servers may still experience delayed plant updates.
[*]Other mods that replace the same farming functions may conflict.
[/list]
